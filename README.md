## What I found interesting about the article

What I found interesting in this article was how much trust gets placed in AI in the military sector, especially considering how sensitive and critical their work is. It was also crazy to see that an AI mistake could pass through so many levels that the U.S. military was actually preparing for an operation before anyone caught it. Also, as a CS student, it comes to show how important testing, verification, and human review is when building software, which is something we're taught about in literally every single class that we take, when industry practices come up in discussion. AI is definitely be a useful tool, but relying on it without properly checking its work, as seen in this article, has the potential for war to break out and lives being lost, in addition to merely making a mistake. Moral of the story is, read the disclaimer that's at the bottom of every AI chatbot: "[this model] is AI and can make mistakes."
